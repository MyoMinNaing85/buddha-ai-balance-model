
Buddha–AI Balance Model

## 1. Background & Intention
This project aims to preserve and translate core Buddha teachings into a modern, practical, and ethical life-management framework using 21st-century AI tools.

The intention is not religious conversion, but **liberation through understanding, balance, and insight**.

---

## 2. Core Foundation: Buddha Teachings
### 2.1 Tipitaka (Three Baskets)
- **Sutta Pitaka**: Real-life teachings, case stories, lessons learned  
- **Vinaya Pitaka**: Ethics, discipline, moral governance  
- **Abhidhamma Pitaka**: Analytical framework of mind and matter  

### 2.2 Four Noble Truths (Analytical Core)
The Four Noble Truths serve as the **root cause analysis (RCA)** model for suffering, behavior, and decision-making.

---

## 3. Practice Framework
### 3.1 Daily Life Application
- Mind & Matter observation
- Purity through awareness
- Cause–effect understanding

### 3.2 MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction)
Used as a **practical tactical layer** for modern life stress and decision-making.

---

## 4. Roadmap: The Noble Eightfold Path
The Eightfold Path is interpreted as a **balanced roadmap**:
- Wisdom
- Ethics
- Mental discipline  

Applied with **insight, not blind belief**.

---

## 5. Integration with 21st Century AI
AI is treated as a **tool**, not authority:
- Pattern recognition
- Reflection assistance
- Ethical decision support

Human insight remains the final authority.

---

## 6. Model Structure
- **Base**: Buddha Teaching  
- **Tool**: AI (21st century)  
- **Lifestyle**: Minimal & mindful  
- **Structure**: Balance  
- **Daily Practice**: MBSR  
- **Governance**: Morality & ethics
